    Meet Nupur

    Nupur was born in Lucknow, Uttar Pradesh, and has been cooking since she got married at 21. Everything on this menu comes from her kitchen, and most of it from memory. Her kitchen is fully vegetarian.

    Her favorite thing to make is chole bhature.

    She's also tired of hearing "kuch bhi" every time she asks what to make. So this site gives her a real answer: pick a dish yourself, or let the Kya banau? button pick for you.
