    Meet Nupur

    Nupur was born in Lucknow, Uttar Pradesh, and has been cooking since she got married. Everything on this menu comes from her kitchen, and most of it from memory. Her kitchen is fully vegetarian.

    Her favorite thing to make is chole bhature.

    Her spice box

    The spices she uses in almost everything she makes. Tap a tin to see it.

    Tap a tin Every spice in her kitchen, one tin at a time.
    Cumin seeds. Usually the first thing into hot oil for the tadka. Turmeric. Bright yellow, goes into most sabzis and dals. Coriander powder. Earthy and mild, the base of many gravies. Red chili powder. Brings the heat and the color. A warm blend, sprinkled in near the end of cooking. Goes in early, and it always gets a taste check at the end.

    She's also tired of hearing "kuch bhi" every time she asks what to make. So this site gives her a real answer: pick a dish yourself, or let the Kya banau? button pick for you.
