# Shaam-e-Awadh

A family menu of vegetarian dishes from Nupur's kitchen, from everyday home-cooked meals to Lucknowi specialties.

**Live site:** https://suhxnitiwari.github.io/shaam-e-awadh/

## What's on the site

- **Kuch Bhi:** answers "what should we make?" by asking what you're craving (main course, appetizer, dessert, drink, namkeen, or anything). For a main course it also asks rice or roti, then picks a dish. "Pick another" re-rolls and "Start over" goes back to the questions.
- **Full menu:** every dish grouped by category, with a Family Favorites section and search.
- **About the Chef:** a short bio of Nupur.

## Edit the menu

All dishes are in the `MENU` list near the bottom of `index.html`, inside the `<script>` tag. Add, remove, or move a dish inside its group. Counts, numbering, and the Kuch Bhi picks update on their own.

Family Favorites is a separate block in the Full menu tab. Edit the four `fav-card` entries there to change it.

## Edit the chef page

The About the Chef text is in the `<article class="chef">` block in `index.html`.

## Main course

The main course section has 51 everyday dinner options, including dals and legumes. The goal is familiar dishes the whole family already eats.
